# Baseline Tests & Progress Checks — IRONMAN Belgium 2027

> These tests establish your starting point and track your progress every 8 weeks. They're simple, repeatable, and give you meaningful data without a lab.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always perform tests under similar conditions: same time of day, similar nutrition, rested (not after a hard training day). A test done when you're fatigued is not a useful baseline.

## Test 1: 400m Pool Swim Time Trial

**Purpose:** Track swim speed and estimate race pacing.

**Protocol:**
1. Swim 200m easy warm-up
2. Rest 3 minutes
3. Swim 400m at best sustainable effort — push hard but don't sprint
4. Record time

## Test 2: 5 km Run Time Trial

**Purpose:** Track running fitness and estimate race run pacing.

**Protocol:**
1. 10 min easy jog warm-up + 4 × 20 sec strides
2. Run 5 km at best sustainable effort on a flat course or track
3. Record time

**How to interpret:**

## Test 3: 30-Minute Bike Effort (FTP Estimate)

**Purpose:** Establish your functional threshold power (FTP) or average HR baseline.

**Protocol:**
1. 15 min warm-up on trainer including 2 × 1 min hard efforts
2. Ride as hard as you can sustain for 30 minutes — consistent effort, not a sprint that fades
3. Record: average power (if available) and average heart rate
4. FTP estimate = average power over 30 min × 0.95

> **If you don't have a power meter:** Record average HR. Compare across tests. If the same effort (same RPE) produces the same HR, your fitness is maintained. If it produces a lower HR over time — your fitness has improved.

## How to Interpret Progress

### Good signs at each check-in:
- 400m swim time improving (by even 15–30 sec = meaningful progress)
- Long ride duration increasing comfortably
- Long run HR lower for the same pace (cardiac efficiency improving)
- Brick sessions: transition discomfort resolving faster each time

### Flags to investigate:
- 5 km run time NOT improving after 16 weeks → check if Z2 running is truly easy enough
- Swim NOT improving after 8 weeks → prioritise drill sessions, consider a swim coach session
- Long ride producing excessive fatigue lasting >2 days → reduce long ride duration by 20%

### What to do at each reassessment

At each 8-week check, ask:
1. Are all three disciplines progressing (even slowly)?
2. Am I recovering well between sessions?
3. Does my plan for the next 8 weeks feel ambitious but realistic?

Adjust the targets in [[../Plan/03 Week by Week Schedule]] accordingly. This is a *living document* — update it as your fitness evolves.
