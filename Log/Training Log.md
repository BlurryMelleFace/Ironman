# Training Log — IRONMAN Belgium 2027

> Log every session here. Consistent logging reveals patterns — when you're fatigued, when your best sessions happen, and which disciplines need attention.

> **Strava/Garmin:** Sync detailed data there. Use this log for quick notes, planned vs. completed, and how you felt. The combination is more powerful than either alone.

---

## How to Log

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Discipline** | Swim / Bike / Run / Brick / Strength |
| **Planned** | What the schedule said (e.g. "60 min Z2 run") |
| **Completed** | What you actually did (e.g. "55 min, felt heavy") |
| **RPE** | Rate of Perceived Exertion: 1 (very easy) → 10 (maximum) |
| **Notes** | Anything relevant — form cues, nutrition, fatigue, highlights |

**RPE Quick Guide:**
- 1–3: Recovery, easy walk/jog, barely working
- 4–5: Zone 2, conversational, comfortable
- 6–7: Zone 3, noticeably harder
- 8–9: Zone 4–5, interval effort, breathing hard
- 10: Maximum, can't hold for long
