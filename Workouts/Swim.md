# Swim Workouts — IRONMAN Belgium 2027

> **Goal:** Build from <30 min sustained swimming to a comfortable 75–80 min (≈3,800m) by race week.
> **Race target:** 3.8 km in ~1:15–1:25 · pace ≈ 2:00–2:15/100m

> [!NOTE]
> **Swim is your biggest limiter and your top priority in Phase 1 and 2.** The good news: 11 months of consistent 3×/week swimming will transform your swimming. Technique matters more than fitness at your current level — an efficient stroke will do more for your swim time than extra metres.

| Level | Description | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Easy / Drill | Pure technique focus | Barely breathing harder |
| Aerobic (Z2) | Sustainable forever, full conversation | Controlled effort |
| Threshold (Z4) | Race effort — hard but sustainable | 2–3 words only |
| Fast / Sprint | Max effort for short distances | Breathless |

## Key Technique Drills (Use in Every Warm-up)

### Catch-Up Drill
- One hand waits extended in front until the other arm completes its pull and also extends forward
- **Purpose:** Improves stroke timing, prevents rushing
- **Cue:** Pause, reach, pull — pause, reach, pull

### Fingertip Drag
- During recovery (arm out of water), drag fingertips along the water surface from hip to entry
- **Purpose:** Teaches high-elbow recovery, prevents wide arm swing
- **Cue:** Keep your elbow high, fingers skim the surface

### Side-Balance Drill
- Kick on your side for 6 kicks, then take one stroke and switch sides
- **Purpose:** Body rotation, balance, reducing drag
- **Cue:** Stack your hips and shoulders, ear in the water

### Single-Arm Drill
- Swim with one arm only — other arm at side or extended. Alternate each 25m
- **Purpose:** Highlights catch and pull mechanics on each side
- **Cue:** Feel the "catch" — your hand should feel water pressure like paddling a canoe

### Sighting Practice (from Phase 3 onward, before open water)
- Every 10 strokes, lift eyes just above waterline to sight a target (not head out of water)
- **Purpose:** Open-water navigation without breaking stroke
- **Cue:** Crocodile eyes — just the eyes above water, not your whole head

## Session Library

### 🏊 Session S1 — Technique Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

**Duration:** 45–55 min | **Volume:** ~900–1,100m | **Phase 1**

```
Warm-up (150m):
  4 × 25m easy freestyle — focus: relaxed breathing, no rushing

Drill Set (300m):
  4 × 75m drill repeats, 20 sec rest:
    - 75m catch-up drill
    - 75m fingertip drag
    - 75m side-balance
    - 75m normal freestyle — apply the drill feeling

Main Set (400–500m):
  4–5 × 100m at aerobic pace, 25 sec rest
  Focus: count strokes per length, aim for 18–22 strokes

Cool-down (100m):
  2 × 50m easy backstroke or head-up breaststroke
```

---

### 🏊 Session S2 — Endurance Builder (Weeks 3–8)

**Duration:** 50–60 min | **Volume:** ~1,200–1,500m | **Phase 1**

```
Warm-up (200m):
  4 × 50m easy, alternate 50m drill / 50m freestyle

Main Set — Option A (intervals):
  8 × 100m at aerobic pace, 20 sec rest
  Focus: consistent pace, same stroke count every length

Main Set — Option B (pyramid):
  100m easy → 200m aerobic → 300m aerobic → 200m aerobic → 100m easy
  30 sec rest between each

Cool-down (100m):
  Easy backstroke
```

---

### 🏊 Session S3 — Volume Build (Weeks 7–13)

**Duration:** 55–65 min | **Volume:** ~1,500–1,800m | **Phase 1–2**

```
Warm-up (200m):
  200m easy freestyle + drills choice

Drill Set (200m):
  4 × 50m: single-arm drill each length

Main Set:
  10 × 100m at aerobic pace, 20 sec rest
  OR
  2 × 500m at comfortable aerobic pace, 45 sec rest

Finishers (100m):
  4 × 25m fast (threshold effort), 30 sec rest each
  Focus: feel the difference between "fast" and "tense"

Cool-down (100m):
  Easy, slow
```

---

### 🏊 Session S4 — Threshold Intervals (Weeks 10–26)

**Duration:** 55–65 min | **Volume:** ~1,800–2,200m | **Phase 1–2**

```
Warm-up (200m):
  Easy + 4 × 25m build (start easy, finish fast)

Pre-set (300m):
  3 × 100m at aerobic pace, 20 sec rest

Main Set — Threshold Intervals:
  6 × 200m at hard aerobic/threshold pace, 30 sec rest
  OR
  4 × 300m at aerobic pace, 25 sec rest
  Focus: hold the same pace each rep — controlled, not dying

Cool-down (200m):
  200m easy with any drill
```

---

### 🏊 Session S5 — Long Aerobic Swim (Weeks 18–40)

**Duration:** 60–75 min | **Volume:** ~2,200–3,000m | **Phase 2–3**

```
Warm-up (300m):
  200m easy + 100m drills choice

Main Set:
  Option A: 1 × 1,500m continuous at steady aerobic pace
  Option B: 3 × 500m at aerobic pace, 45 sec rest
  Option C: 6 × 400m at aerobic pace, 30 sec rest
  
  Focus on: maintaining form under fatigue — this is where most people slow down dramatically.
  Key cue: if your stroke rate drops and arms start slapping, slow down slightly and reset.

Finishers (200m):
  4 × 50m threshold pace, 30 sec rest

Cool-down (200m):
  200m easy backstroke
```

---

### 🏊 Session S6 — Race-Specific Endurance (Weeks 35–49)

**Duration:** 65–80 min | **Volume:** ~3,000–3,800m | **Phase 3–4**

```
Warm-up (400m):
  200m easy + 4 × 50m drills choice

Main Set (race simulation):
  1 × 3,000m continuous, aerobic effort
  OR
  2 × 1,500m at aerobic pace, 2 min rest
  
  Practice sighting every 10 strokes (even in pool — pick a spot on the wall).
  Practice wetsuit swimming where possible.
  Practice drafting if training with others.

Cool-down (200–400m):
  Very easy, breath-focus
```

---

### 🌊 Open Water Sessions (from May 2027, Phase 3–4)

**Start:** Week 32 (mid-May 2027) | **Frequency:** 1×/week replacing one pool session

```
Session OW1 — Orientation (Weeks 32–34):
  Warm-up: 5 min easy, tread water
  Main: 2–3 × 400m loops, rest between, focus on sighting
  Focus: comfort, wetsuit feel, no lane lines
  Safety: always swim with a buddy or at a supervised venue

Session OW2 — Building Confidence (Weeks 35–40):
  2–3 × 600–800m with sighting practice
  Time yourself on one rep for comparison with pool

Session OW3 — Race-Specific (Weeks 41–45):
  1 × 2,000–3,000m continuous at race effort
  Simulate race start: sprint first 200m, then settle into aerobic pace
  Practice mass start feel if group training available
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Safety rules for open water:**
> - Never swim alone
> - Use a tow-float / swim buoy for visibility
> - Know the water temperature — wetsuit is mandatory below 24°C in IRONMAN events
> - Familiarise yourself with the course at Knokke-Heist before race day if possible

|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (Oct–Dec) | 900 → 1,800m | S1 (drills), S2 (intervals), S3 (volume) |

| Phase 3 (Apr–Jul) | 2,600 → 3,000m | S5, S6, OW starts |
| Phase 4 (Jul–Aug) | 3,000 → 3,800m | S6, OW race-specific |
| Taper (Aug–Sep) | 2,500 → 1,500m | Short sharpeners |
