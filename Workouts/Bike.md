# Bike Workouts — IRONMAN Belgium 2027

> **Race bike leg:** 180 km · Target: ~5:30–6:00 h · Average ~30–33 km/h
> **Your strength:** You already ride 4+ hours — the bike is your best discipline. The goal is to ride it *efficiently* so you have legs for the marathon.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The #1 Ironman bike mistake is going too hard.** Your race target is an aerobic effort (Zone 2), not a fast effort. You should feel like you're holding back. Training will teach you what that feels like.

---

## Intensity Reference (Bike)

| Zone | Description | Talk Test | If using trainer power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 — Recovery | Very easy spin | Full conversation, barely noticing | <55% FTP |
| Z2 — Aerobic | Comfortable, sustainable | Full comfortable sentences | 56–75% FTP |
| Z3 — Tempo | Moderate, noticeably harder | Short phrases | 76–87% FTP |
| Z4 — Threshold | Hard, sustained effort | 2–3 words | 88–95% FTP |
| Z5 — VO2max | Very hard, short bursts | Cannot speak | >95% FTP |

> **FTP Estimate:** If you don't have a power meter, use RPE and the talk test. If you do use a smart trainer, do a 20-min FTP test: ride 20 min at your best sustained power. FTP ≈ average power × 0.95.

## Session Library

### 🚴 Session B1 — Aerobic Foundation (All phases)

**Duration:** 1:30–4:00 h | **Intensity:** Z2 | **Use:** Long ride on trainer or road

```
Warm-up (15 min):
  Easy spin, build from Z1 to Z2 gradually
  Cadence: 85–90 rpm — don't push big gears

Main Set:
  Sustained Z2 aerobic riding
  - Phase 1 target: 60–90 min main set
  - Phase 2 target: 90–150 min main set
  - Phase 4 target: 180–270 min main set
  
  Every 20–30 min:
    - Take a gel/bar (practice race nutrition from Phase 2 onward)
    - Check in: can you comfortably complete a sentence? If no → ease up
  
  Cadence focus: 85–95 rpm. Avoid grinding big gears — high cadence is more efficient and spares your legs for the run.

Cool-down (10–15 min):
  Easy spin, progressively lighter
```

**Key cue:** If your heart rate drifts above Z2 on a flat road (common in heat), reduce power slightly — don't force pace.

### 🚴 Session B2 — Sweet Spot Intervals (Phase 2 onwards)

**Duration:** 1:30–2:00 h | **Intensity:** Z2 with Z3 blocks | **Use:** Mid-week key session

> *Sweet spot (high Z2/low Z3) is the most time-efficient aerobic stimulus. Use sparingly — this is one of your 20% hard sessions.*

```
Warm-up (15 min):
  Easy spin

Main Set (choose one):
  Option A — Muscular Endurance:
    3 × 15 min at Z3 (short phrases only), 5 min Z2 recovery between
  
  Option B — Over/Under:
    6 × (4 min Z3 / 2 min Z2) — alternating, no full rest
  
  Option C — Tempo Blocks:
    2 × 20 min Z3, 8 min Z2 recovery

Cool-down (15 min):
  Easy Z1 spin
```

### 🚴 Session B3 — Cadence & Efficiency Drills

**Duration:** 1:00–1:30 h | **Intensity:** Z2 | **Use:** Any week — especially useful in winter

```
Warm-up (10 min):
  Easy spin

Drill Set (30 min):
  5 × (3 min high cadence 100–110 rpm / 2 min normal 88–92 rpm)
  Focus: smooth, quiet pedal stroke at high RPM — avoid bouncing in saddle

Main Set (20–30 min):
  Low cadence force work: 3 × 5 min at 65–70 rpm, Z2 effort
  This builds leg strength without the gym — simulates hill climbing
  
Cadence return (10 min):
  Back to 88–92 rpm at Z2

Cool-down (10 min):
  Easy
```

### 🚴 Session B4 — Simulated Race Effort (Phase 4)

**Duration:** 4:00–5:30 h | **Intensity:** Race pace (Z2, disciplined) | **Use:** Long ride in peak weeks

```
Warm-up (20 min):
  Easy Z1–Z2

Main Set (200–300 min):
  Sustained Z2 at race effort
  Think: "I could do this for another 4 hours"
  
  Nutrition protocol:
    - First 20 min: don't eat (simulate race — eat at transition setup, not immediately)
    - Then: fuel every 20–25 min with gels/bars + fluids
    - Target: 60–80g carbohydrate per hour
    - Alternate sweet and neutral flavours to avoid palate fatigue
  
  Pacing check:
    - At 60 min: effort still feels controlled?
    - At 120 min: can you still talk comfortably?
    - At 180 min: if you feel like pushing harder — don't. Save it.

Cool-down (20 min):
  Easy Z1 spin
  Change into run gear for brick session immediately after (see [[Brick]])
```

### 🚴 Session B5 — Hill / Power Repeats (Phase 2 onward, outdoor)

**Duration:** 1:30–2:00 h | **Intensity:** Z2 with Z4 hills | **Use:** Strength building, spring/summer

```
Warm-up (15 min):
  Easy spin

Hill Repeats (30–40 min):
  Find a 3–5 min climb
  5–8 × climb at Z4 effort (hard but controlled — not sprinting)
  Recover fully (Z1) on descent before next effort

Active Recovery Ride (30–40 min):
  Z1–Z2 easy spinning, legs recovering

Cool-down (10 min):
  Easy
```

### 🚴 Session B6 — Thursday Bike Intervals (every Thursday)

**Duration:** 35–105 min | **Intensity:** Z2 with Z3 blocks | **Use:** Every Thursday, followed straight away by the run intervals in [[Run]] R6

```
Warm-up (10–15 min):
  Easy spin, build from Z1 to Z2

Main Set (exact sets are in the day's plan):
  Phase 1:    3 × 3 min → 3 × 10 min at Z3, 3–4 min Z2 between
  Phase 2–3:  3 × 10–15 min or 2 × 20 min at Z3, 4–8 min Z2 between
  Phase 4:    3 × 15–20 min at Z3, 5 min Z2 between
  Recovery / taper weeks: 3 × 2–3 min at Z3, 3 min easy between

Cool-down:
  A few minutes easy, then change shoes for the run intervals
```

**Key cue:** Z3 means short phrases only. Do not push into Z4 — the run intervals follow immediately.

## Race Pacing Strategy

> This is the most important bike advice for your race.

### The "Start Slow, Finish Strong" principle *(coaching consensus across TrainingPeaks, IRONMAN guides)*

At 91 kg, you have the power to ride 180 km at a pace that will destroy your marathon. **The bike leg must be ridden conservatively.**

**Target feel:** First 60 km — holds you back. Middle 60 km — comfortable rhythm. Last 60 km — slightly harder but still aerobic.

| Race segment | Zone | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 km (leaving T1) | Z1–Z2 | Deliberately easy — heart rate elevated from swim, let it settle |
| 30–120 km | Z2 | Full sentences, controlled effort |
| 120–160 km | Z2 | Maintain form, fuel carefully |
| 160–180 km | Z2–Z3 | Controlled push to T2 — not a sprint |

**Critical:** If you feel great at 80 km — that's not a green light to go harder. It means your pacing is perfect. Stay patient.

## Equipment & Setup Tips

- **Tyre pressure:** Check before every ride. Lower pressure (90–100 psi road, 60–70 psi wider tyres) = better comfort on long rides
- **Bike fit:** Before Phase 2 begins (January), consider a professional bike fit. A correct position prevents injuries on long rides and improves aerobic efficiency
- **Aero position:** On the road bike, practise riding on the drops or in a more aero position on trainer to build tolerance. Long-distance cycling in a bad position is exhausting
- **Nutrition carry system:** In Phase 2, set up your bottle system. Know how many bottles/calories you can carry for 180 km
- **Trainer sessions:** Use a fan — heat management on the trainer is essential. Sweat rate is higher indoors
