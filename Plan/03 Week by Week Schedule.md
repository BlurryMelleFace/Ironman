# Week-by-Week Schedule — IRONMAN Belgium 2027

> **Weeks 1–4 are fully scripted daily sessions below.**
> Weeks 5–53 show the template and targets — adjust as your fitness develops.
> Review and reassess at every 8-week benchmark test.

> [!NOTE]
> **How to read intensity:** Z2 = fully conversational (full sentences, controlled breathing). Z4 = hard (few words only). See [[00 Training Plan Overview]] for full zone guide.

## 🟢 PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION

### Week 1 (Oct 2–8, 2026) — Getting Started
**Target: ~6 hours · Introduce the routine**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This week is about assessment and building habits. Complete both baseline tests (swim TT and run TT). Don't be disheartened by the numbers — they're your starting point, not a verdict.

#### Thursday, October 2 — Day 1
**🏊 Swim: Baseline Assessment + Technique Introduction**
- **Duration:** 45–55 min
- **Location:** Pool
- **Warm-up:** 4 × 25m easy freestyle with 20 sec rest — focus only on relaxed breathing
- **Main set:**
  - 400m time trial at best sustainable effort — note your time
  - Rest 5 min
  - 4 × 100m drills:
    - 100m catch-up drill (one arm extends fully before other pulls)
    - 100m fingertip drag (drag fingertips on recovery)
    - 100m side kick (kick on side for 6 kicks, pull, repeat)
    - 100m normal freestyle — apply the feeling from drills
- **Cool-down:** 100m easy backstroke or gentle freestyle
- **Total distance:** ~900–1,000m
- **Purpose:** Establish your swim baseline and introduce basic technique concepts. The 400m TT time goes into your benchmark table on [[../Dashboard]].
- **RPE target:** TT at 8/10, drills at 3–4/10

#### Friday, October 3
**REST** — German Unity Day falls Saturday so no public holiday today. Easy walk if desired.

#### Saturday, October 4
**🚴 Bike: Aerobic Base + Baseline Check**
- **Duration:** 1:45–2:00 h
- **Intensity:** Z2 throughout — if you drift above "full comfortable conversation", back off
- **Set-up:** Smart turbo trainer. Set it to resistance mode or Erg mode at a low wattage if you have a power estimate.
- **Warm-up:** 10 min easy spin, cadence 85–90 rpm
- **Main set:** 75–90 min steady Z2 riding. Aim for a consistent cadence of 85–95 rpm. If using a smart trainer with power, note your average power for future reference.
- **Cool-down:** 10 min easy spin
- **Purpose:** Establish your aerobic bike baseline. Note average HR and any power data.
- **RPE target:** 5–6/10 throughout — if it ever feels like work, you're too hard

#### Sunday, October 5
**🏃 Run: Baseline 5 km Time Trial**
- **Duration:** ~35–50 min total
- **Warm-up:** 10 min very easy jog + 4 × 20 sec strides (short accelerations), walk back
- **Main set:** 5 km time trial — run at your best sustainable effort. Use a flat route or track if available. Note your time.
- **Cool-down:** 10 min easy jog + stretch (calves, hip flexors, hamstrings)
- **Purpose:** Establish your run baseline. This goes into your benchmark table.
- **RPE:** Warm-up 3/10 · TT 8–9/10 · Cool-down 2/10

---

#### Monday, October 6
**💪 Strength: Adaptation Session A**
- **Duration:** 50–60 min
- **Location:** Gym
- **Rest:** 90 seconds between sets

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 3 × 12 | Light DB or KB. Feet shoulder width, hips below parallel, chest tall |
| Romanian Deadlift (RDL) | 3 × 12 | Light barbell or DBs. Hinge at hips, soft knee, feel hamstring stretch |
| Split squat (Bulgarian or basic) | 3 × 10 each leg | Slow and controlled. 3 sec down, 1 sec up |
| Lat pulldown (or band pull-down) | 3 × 12 | Pull elbows to ribs, chest tall |
| Push-up | 3 × 10–15 | Full range. Elevate hands if needed to maintain form |
| Pallof press | 3 × 10 each side | Use cable or band. Resist rotation. Core tension throughout |
| Single-leg calf raise | 2 × 15 each | Slow and full range — Achilles and calf resilience |

> **Choosing weight:** Use a weight where the last 2–3 reps are effortful but your form stays perfect. You should never feel like you're going to fail. Increase weight by the smallest increment when all reps feel easy.

#### Tuesday, October 7
**🏊 Swim: Technique Session**
- **Duration:** 45 min
- **Warm-up:** 4 × 50m easy with 20 sec rest
- **Main set:**
  - 6 × 50m drill: alternating single-arm drill (one arm extends, other at side) with 25 sec rest
  - 4 × 100m with focus cue: count strokes per length, aim to reduce by 1 per length
  - 200m continuous easy freestyle — apply efficient stroke
- **Cool-down:** 100m easy
- **Total:** ~1,100m
- **RPE:** 3–5/10

#### Wednesday, October 8
**🏃 Run: Easy Aerobic Run**
- **Duration:** 45 min
- **Intensity:** Z2 — you should be able to hold a conversation the entire time. If you can't, slow down or walk briefly.
- **Warm-up:** Included — first 10 min deliberately slower
- **Main:** 30 min steady Z2
- **Cool-down:** 5 min easy walk
- **Purpose:** First of your consistent mid-week easy runs. These feel too easy — that's correct.
- **RPE:** 4–5/10
